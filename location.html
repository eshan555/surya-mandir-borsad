Getting Here

◈

By Road

The mandir sits directly on Tarapur–Vasad Road (State Highway 6), making it an easy stop for those travelling between Anand and Vadodara.

By Rail

Borsad railway station (on the Vasad–Kathana line) is closest, with Anand Junction — a major stop on the Western Railway network — about 18 km away.

By Air

Vadodara Airport (~40 km) is the closest, with Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport, Ahmedabad, also within reach (~90–100 km).
