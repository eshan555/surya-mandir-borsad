Visiting Tips

◈
🏛️

Best Time to Visit

Mornings around Mangala or Shangar Aarti, and evenings around Sandhya Aarti, are the most atmospheric times to visit.

🍽️

Food & Stay

The mandir complex includes a guest house with AC and Non‑AC rooms, and a food facility for visiting pilgrims.

🚗

Parking & Families

On‑site parking and a children's play area make it comfortable to visit with family for a longer stay.

Surya Mandir Borsad
