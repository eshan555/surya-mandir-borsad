Welcome

◈

Built in 1972 following a remarkable story of divine manifestation, Surya Mandir Borsad has grown into one of Gujarat's best‑loved temples to Lord Surya Narayan — drawing pilgrims from across the country and abroad for daily darshan, aarti, yagyas and community seva.
