Frequently Asked Questions

◈

What are the darshan timings at Surya Mandir Borsad?

The temple is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, all 7 days of the week. There are five aartis a day: Mangala (6:00 AM), Shangar (7:00 AM), Rajbhog (11:00 AM), Sandhya (6:45 PM) and Shayan (9:15 PM).

Is entry to Surya Mandir Borsad free?

Yes, entry is free and photography is permitted throughout the premises.

Where is Surya Mandir Borsad located?

The temple is on Tarapur–Vasad Road (State Highway 6), Borsad, Anand District, Gujarat 388540 — about 18 km from Anand, 40 km from Vadodara and 90–100 km from Ahmedabad.

What is the phone number for Surya Mandir Borsad?

The mandir can be reached at 02696 222215.

Does the temple offer food and lodging for pilgrims?

Yes. The mandir complex includes a guest house with AC and Non‑AC rooms and a food facility for visiting pilgrims, alongside on-site parking and a children's play area.
